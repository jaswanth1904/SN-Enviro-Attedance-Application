2. Render Starter Plan (~₹590/Month) — Permanent 60-Second Fix

Why the Free Tier was failing with 503:

Render Free Tier automatically puts containers to sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity. Booting up takes 50 to 90 seconds. External pingers like UptimeRobot and cron-job.org time out after 30 seconds and get blocked by Cloudflare's bot filter, resulting in 503 Service Unavailable errors.

What Render Starter ($7 / month = ~₹588 INR / month) Delivers:

  • Always-On Container: Runs 24/7/365 without ever sleeping or spinning down.
  • Zero 503 Errors: Every API call answers in < 30 milliseconds round the clock.
  • Real-Time Live Map: WebSockets stay connected continuously with zero dropped telemetry.
  • No Ping Bots Needed: You can completely turn off and delete cron-job.org and UptimeRobot.

4. Live GPS Tracking: Web Browser vs. Uber / Swiggy Model

"If employees are traveling, will they be visible on the live map if they clear the app from the background?"

A. The Web Browser (Current State) — NO:

When an employee uses Chrome or Safari on their phone, the GPS works only while the website is active on screen. As soon as they lock the phone or clear the browser tab, Android and iOS freeze the browser's JavaScript engine to protect battery life. Web browsers cannot track location in the background.

B. The Native Mobile App (Uber / Swiggy Model) — YES:

In the native Android app, we use an Android Foreground Service. When an engineer checks in at 9:15 AM:

  • A persistent status bar notification appears: "📍 SN Enviro Field Tracking Active • Shift In Progress".
  • The background engine captures high-accuracy GPS coordinates every 30 seconds and pings the live map.
  • What if the user clears the app from Recent Apps? The service has the START_STICKY flag. The Android OS automatically restarts the tracking engine within 2 seconds. Telemetry only stops when the engineer clicks Check-Out.
